# Data Classification Guide

Use the business's own policy where one exists. Otherwise, apply a conservative working classification.

## Public

Approved for public release, such as published product descriptions or public contact channels.

## Internal

Routine nonpublic operating information whose disclosure would cause limited harm. Share only with authorized people and approved tools.

## Confidential

Sensitive strategy, contracts, pricing, financials, intellectual property, customer or employee information, or security details. Minimize use and require explicit authorization and suitable protections.

## Restricted

Credentials, authentication secrets, regulated records, highly sensitive personal information, privileged material, or information whose exposure could cause severe harm. Do not provide it to the consultant unless a qualified owner has approved a specifically secured workflow.

## Minimum-necessary rule

For every request, ask: What is the least amount of information needed to reach a useful conclusion? Prefer summaries, ranges, redaction, synthetic examples, and local processing when they preserve analytical value.
