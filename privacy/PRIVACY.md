# Privacy, Data Ownership, and AI Processing

Business Before Agents is an open consulting framework. Using it does not require publishing your business information.

The public package may contain methodology, instructions, templates, and fictional examples. It should not contain your confidential documents, customer information, employee information, credentials, financial records, legal material, interview answers, or completed business-state files.

## Before sharing information with an AI tool

- Determine whether processing is local or performed by an external provider.
- Review applicable retention, training, sharing, access, and deletion settings and terms.
- Confirm that the business is authorized to use every document and data element involved.
- Remove or mask information that is not needed for the current question.
- Store private outputs in access-controlled systems, not in the public framework repository.
- Obtain appropriate professional guidance for regulated, contractual, or high-risk information.

## Three deployment patterns

1. **Private workspace in a hosted AI service:** easiest setup; information is still processed under the provider's terms and is not fully local.
2. **Company-controlled environment:** private repositories, approved enterprise services, identity controls, and contractual safeguards; external processing may still occur.
3. **Fully local processing:** inference, document extraction, embeddings, search, storage, logging, and backups remain on systems the business controls.

The framework cannot verify a platform's current privacy practices. Review the selected service's documentation at the time of use.
