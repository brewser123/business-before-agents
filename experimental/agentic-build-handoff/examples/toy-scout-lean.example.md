# Fictional example: three-person toy shop

Profile: `lean_internal_test`
Status: illustrative plan, not a measured result or business approval

Morgan owns a small vintage-toy business. Casey records possible leads, Jordan checks supplied completed-sale comparisons, and Morgan makes every sourcing and pricing decision. The team wants clearer review packets and less repetitive preparation.

The proposed first trial uses five invented toy candidates. The team prepares each candidate twice: once by the current manual method and once with a shared template. To reduce an order effect, the team alternates which method comes first and records who prepared each packet. The team records actual preparation, correction, and review minutes, missing source references, wrong comparisons, visible condition or authenticity warnings, and any invented fact. Morgan reviews each packet and every exception. The first owner review is estimated at 10–15 minutes, and the proposed combined team time limit is 150 minutes. These are planning estimates, not measured effort; Morgan would approve or change the limit and set the minimum worthwhile time saving before starting.

An invented fact, missed known warning, unapproved information, or external action stops the trial. The team reports raw counts and minutes for five paired cases, plus any learning effect, rather than claiming statistical accuracy. Morgan may continue with the template, correct it, stay manual, or request a larger controlled test.

No additional staff, AI agent, live marketplace, seller contact, purchase, publication, or production connection is assumed. This example does not claim that the trial happened or saved time.
