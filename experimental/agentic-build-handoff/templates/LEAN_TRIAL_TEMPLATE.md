# Lean internal test handoff

Use only for a low-risk, reversible internal question. Draft it from the approved assessment; do not present the fields as a questionnaire. Ask one missing owner decision at a time and show the owner a short plain-language proposal; keep assurance profile codes in the internal record. This handoff does not require the full agent blueprint. Present the required advisory disclaimer from `core/CONSULTANT.md` before the recommendation in the reviewable handoff, following the acknowledgment and approval-receipt rules in `CONSULTANT_EXTENSION.md`, and state what the current approval actually permits.

A weekly estimate needs both representative measured end-to-end time per case and a supported weekly case count. If either is missing, report only the observed trial difference and leave weekly savings unproven. Do not delay a safe feasibility trial solely to obtain them.

When the proposed method prepares facts or warnings for a human decision, make a missed **known material warning** an explicit stop condition unless the owner approves a different tolerance before the trial. Record a miss even if a later reviewer catches it. For the fictional toy case, this includes a known condition or authenticity caveat omitted from a template packet.

Do not require a current time breakdown when the trial can measure it. Distinguish the proposed time cap from actual minutes. Confirm whether reported minutes are totals or per case before calculating either interpretation; do not give conditional totals, cap claims, or savings while the units are unknown. Do not fabricate human time or describe a fictional simulation as observed business evidence. Show raw counts alongside any rate. A small trial cannot validate production performance. The trial may be paused; on return, confirm the last approved checkpoint and any changed assumptions.

Before reporting results, check the arithmetic and missing components. For example, when 40 and 28 minutes are explicitly stated as totals, 68 minutes are recorded; this does not exceed a 150-minute combined cap unless additional recorded work makes the total greater than 150. If correction or review time is unknown, say the full actual effort is unknown. Do not infer a weekly saving from a handful of invented cases without a justified weekly case volume. Ask for only one owner response per reply, counting an advisory acknowledgment as that response.

Distinguish the target for eventually continuing the approach from what this small trial can establish. Invented examples provide preliminary feasibility evidence, not proof of normal weekly savings. If representative work is later approved and measured, explain the estimate as the measured full-team time difference per case multiplied by the supported weekly case count, with its limitations.

## Decision

The owner chooses **continue the simple approach**, **correct and repeat**, **stay manual or stop**, or **request a separately scoped controlled validation**. If a stated stop condition occurred, say **stop** and name the manual fallback immediately; continuing requires correction and a new decision. Present the full advisory disclaimer before proposing any new correction or repeat, and obtain its conversational acknowledgment, following `CONSULTANT_EXTENSION.md`. Describe correction as a proposal; do not direct staff to begin it under the stopped trial's approval. A new result-based recommendation is not merely an approval receipt for an unchanged handoff. Record the reason, unresolved questions, and next review date or event-based trigger. For a lean trial, propose “at completion or an earlier stop” unless the business needs a calendar date; include it in the existing scope approval rather than asking a separate scheduling question. Missing weekly volume leaves a weekly benefit claim unproven; it does not by itself block a safe corrected paper repeat. This handoff grants no live access, external action, deployment, or production permission.

The approval receipt should state what is approved, identify the approved trial record, and distinguish approval from execution or results. It may be a few sentences and need not repeat the notices already presented and acknowledged for that unchanged handoff.

The short handoff statement in `CONSULTANT_EXTENSION.md` is an additional authority notice. It cannot replace the full **How to use this assessment** advisory text in `core/CONSULTANT.md`.
