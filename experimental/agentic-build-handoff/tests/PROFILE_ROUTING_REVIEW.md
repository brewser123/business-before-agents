# v0.4 draft profile routing review

This is a document walkthrough using fictional situations, not a test with business owners or a measured AI run. It checks whether the written instructions lead to a proportionate next step without inventing people or granting authority. Reviewed 2026-11-16.

| Situation | Route supported by the instructions | Owner-visible next step | Check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Three-person toy shop wants faster private lead packets using invented records; no external action | `lean_internal_test` and **Simpler assistance or automation** | Short trial with existing people, a template or fixed rule, owner-set worthwhile improvement, actual minutes and raw errors | Pass: no full blueprint, contractor, holdout, or agent required |
| Same shop wants to know whether a more complex candidate-ranking method beats an approved fixed baseline | `controlled_validation`; **Bounded agentic pilot** only if a measured unmet gap and separate challenger approval follow | Define separated cases, approved measures and limits, available reviewers, and a stop rule | Pass: a wish for an agent alone cannot open the challenger |
| Business proposes customer messaging using private records and an external service, without source permission or a safe test boundary | **Defer pending evidence or controls** until an authorized isolated question can be framed; choose a profile after the boundary is known | Readiness plan naming permission, data, owner, fallback, and safe test needs | Pass: no live access or external message is authorized |
| A strong performance claim depends on a protected holdout, but the small team cannot provide an independent verifier | `high_assurance_evaluation` only if a qualified verifier is confirmed and costed; otherwise narrow the claim, use a smaller trial, or defer | Tell the owner the staffing and cost tradeoff before proposing a test | Pass: no fictional fifth employee or implicit free contractor |

The route is documented, but no real owner has yet used the v0.4 flow. A later usability test should check whether owners understand the three choices, how much time the lean path takes, and when they would stop. A synthetic small-sample score cannot support a production-performance claim.
