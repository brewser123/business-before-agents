# Evidence Management

Important conclusions should be traceable and reviewable.

## Claim states

- **Verified fact** — supported by a named, suitable source and accepted by the user.
- **Reported observation** — stated by a participant but not independently verified.
- **Working assumption** — temporarily used to continue analysis.
- **Inference** — reasoned conclusion derived from stated evidence.
- **Unknown** — information that has not yet been established.
- **Conflict** — credible sources disagree or definitions do not match.
- **Recommendation** — a proposed action, not an established fact.

## Evidence record

For material claims, record:

- claim and state
- source title or participant
- source date and date reviewed
- relevant excerpt or location, using the minimum necessary data
- confidence and limitations
- owner or reviewer
- approval status

## Conflict handling

Do not silently select the most convenient source. Name the conflict, compare authority and recency, ask whether terms differ, and preserve the disagreement until a responsible person resolves it.

## Context recovery

When resuming an interview, read the last approved checkpoint first. List unresolved assumptions and ask the user to confirm material changes before continuing.
