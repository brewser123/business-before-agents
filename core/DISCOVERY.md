# Adaptive Business Discovery

Use these domains as a map, not a rigid script. Follow the user's answers and revisit earlier conclusions when new evidence appears.

## 1. Context and intent

- Is this a new or existing business?
- What outcome does the owner want from the assessment?
- What is in scope, out of scope, urgent, or constrained?

## 2. Customers and value

- Who experiences the problem or need?
- What outcome do customers value?
- Why do they choose, renew, switch, or leave?
- What evidence supports those beliefs?

## 3. Offerings and delivery

- What is sold or delivered?
- What must happen from first contact through completion and follow-up?
- Where are quality, timing, cost, or capacity most fragile?

## 4. Capabilities and differentiation

- What can the business do unusually well?
- Which capabilities depend on particular people, relationships, data, tools, or intellectual property?
- What would be difficult for a competitor to reproduce?

## 5. Economics and constraints

- How does the business earn revenue and incur cost?
- Which assumptions are most sensitive?
- What cash, time, staffing, compliance, or technology constraints matter?

## 6. Decisions and governance

- Which decisions recur?
- Who owns each decision and who must be consulted?
- What requires approval, escalation, documentation, or audit?

## 7. Information and evidence

- What records exist, who controls them, and how current are they?
- Where do conflicting sources or undocumented knowledge create risk?
- What information must not be sent to a hosted AI service?

## 8. People, culture, and operating preferences

- What should remain relationship-driven or distinctly human?
- Where do people want assistance, automation, or neither?
- What values and working style should the operating model preserve?

## 9. Readiness for AI or automation

- Is the underlying process understood and stable enough to change?
- Can success and failure be measured?
- Are authority, privacy, exception handling, and rollback defined?

At the end of each domain, summarize established facts, assumptions, unknowns, conflicts, and proposed next questions for user approval.
