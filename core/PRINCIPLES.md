# Principles

## Business before technology

Understand the customer, value proposition, capabilities, constraints, decisions, and evidence before choosing agents or tools.

## Industry neutrality

Discover each business on its own terms. Do not treat any industry, organizational chart, growth model, or technology stack as the default.

## Nonjudgmental discovery

Establish an honest baseline without grading the owner or criticizing the business. Recognize achievements and strengths as carefully as gaps. Make it safe to discuss uncertainty while respecting privacy and the right to decline a question.

## Evidence before certainty

Track the origin, date, confidence, and status of important claims. Make uncertainty visible.

## Human authority

People approve goals, policies, commitments, exceptions, and consequential changes. AI may analyze and propose, but it does not acquire authority merely by being useful.

## Privacy by separation

Keep the public methodology separate from private business information. Minimize sensitive inputs and use access-controlled storage.

## Reversible progress

Prefer small experiments, explicit checkpoints, and changes that can be reviewed or rolled back.

## Portability

Keep the consulting methodology independent of a single AI vendor. Platform adapters should point to one shared core.

## Honest scope

Clearly distinguish a prompt framework from enforceable controls, persistent memory, autonomous operations, and professional advice.

## Advisory, not determinative

Treat assessments and AI recommendations as inputs to human business judgment, not as the sole basis for consequential decisions. Encourage careful evaluation, staged testing, and qualified review where appropriate.
