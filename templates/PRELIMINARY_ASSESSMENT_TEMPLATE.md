# Preliminary Business Assessment

## How to use this assessment

This is a free, limited-scope service and should be understood in that context. It provides a suggested model designed to help a business begin or improve its AI and agentic path, especially when the owner is new to AI. It supports thoughtful planning and aims to improve the likelihood of useful outcomes; it is not a comprehensive professional engagement and does not guarantee success.

The business must evaluate every proposed use case closely, including its people, customers, finances, operations, data, privacy, security, legal obligations, and risk tolerance. Recommendations should be tested carefully, introduced in appropriate stages, and reviewed by qualified people where needed. Do not rely solely on AI or this assessment to make consequential business decisions. The business and its authorized decision-makers remain responsible for whether and how to proceed.

If a decision creates substantial financial exposure or the owner has serious financial concerns, use this assessment only as an initial baseline and seek a deeper review from appropriately qualified financial, legal, operational, security, or business professionals. The free baseline provided may also be used to begin a separate external paid consultative engagement that examines the business plan, evidence, risks, and recommendations in greater depth. Any paid engagement must define its own scope, provider qualifications, fees, deliverables, and terms. There is no obligation to purchase a paid service.
