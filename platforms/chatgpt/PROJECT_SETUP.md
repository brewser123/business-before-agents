# ChatGPT Project Setup

Interface names and data practices can change; verify the current product documentation and account settings before use.

1. Create a private project dedicated to one business assessment.
2. Review `privacy/PRIVACY.md` and choose what information may be processed in this environment.
3. Add the files in `core/` as project instructions or reference knowledge, within current platform limits.
4. Keep a private copy of `templates/BUSINESS_STATE_TEMPLATE.md` for approved checkpoints.
5. Start a new conversation with: **“Start the Business Before Agents discovery interview. Ask one question at a time and separate facts, assumptions, unknowns, and recommendations.”**
6. Periodically export or copy approved checkpoints to storage the business controls.
7. To stop, say: **“Pause and create my resume checkpoint.”** Save the approved checkpoint with the private business-state record.
8. To continue later, reopen the project and say: **“Resume from my last approved checkpoint.”** If ChatGPT cannot retrieve it, attach or paste the saved checkpoint.

Project context can make returning easier, but do not rely on conversational memory as the authoritative record. The approved checkpoint is the continuity record. Do not upload confidential information until the responsible business owner has reviewed the account's current privacy, retention, and training settings.
